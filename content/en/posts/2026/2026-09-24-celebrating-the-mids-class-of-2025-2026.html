The MIDS LL.M. programme in International Dispute Settlement proudly celebrated the graduation of its 2025-2026 cohort on 24 September 2026 at the Maison de la Paix in Geneva. Bringing together students from around the world, the ceremony marked the culmination of an intensive academic year dedicated to excellence in international dispute resolution. The event featured addresses from distinguished members of the MIDS community, a keynote speech by Gourab Banerji SA, and the presentation of the prestigious Gabrielle Kaufmann-Kohler Award.

On Thursday, 24 September 2026, the MIDS
community gathered at the Maison de la Paix to celebrate the
achievements of the Class of 2025-2026. After a year of rigorous study,
intellectual challenge, and professional growth, our graduates joined the
distinguished global network of MIDS alumni who continue to shape the practice
and scholarship of international dispute settlement around the world. The ceremony opened with welcome remarks by Emilie Poisson , MIDS Executive Director, followed by congratulatory messages from:


Professor
Alexandre Flückiger, Dean of the Faculty of Law, University of Geneva
Professor
Nico Krisch, Head of the International Law Department, Geneva Graduate
Institute
Professor
Zachary Douglas KC , MIDS and CIDS Director
The celebration also featured reflections from Ashley Chandler , speaking on behalf of the graduating class, and congratulatory
remarks from Anindya Basarkod , representing the MIDS Alumni Association.

 

Spotlight: Gourab Banerji's Keynote address

A highlight of the ceremony was the keynote
speech delivered by Gourab Banerji SA , President of the Arbitration Bar
of India and Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators.

In his address, "Arbitrating in the Age
of Disruption," Mr Banerji examined how technological advances,
shifting economic realities, geopolitical developments, and changing
expectations of users are transforming the practice of international
arbitration. Drawing on his extensive experience as counsel and arbitrator, he
offered graduates valuable insights into the challenges and opportunities
facing the next generation of dispute resolution practitioners.

His thought-provoking reflections reminded
graduates that adaptability, intellectual curiosity, and professional integrity
will remain essential qualities for success in an increasingly dynamic global
landscape.

Honouring academic excellence
The ceremony also featured the presentation of
the Gabrielle Kaufmann-Kohler Award , recognizing outstanding academic
achievement within the graduating class.

The award was presented by Professor Pierre
Tercier , who shared remarks celebrating the exceptional quality of the
students' work and the continued commitment of the MIDS community to academic
excellence.

A celebration of achievement and community

The presentation of graduates was a
particularly memorable moment of the ceremony, reflecting not only the academic
accomplishments of the Class of 2025-2026 but also the friendships,
collaborations, and professional connections forged throughout the programme.
As the ceremony concluded, Professor Zachary Douglas KC offered closing
reflections on the graduates' future role in advancing the rule of law and
contributing to the continued development of international dispute settlement.
Graduates, faculty, alumni, family members, and friends then gathered for an
aperitif at the Pavillon Barton, continuing the celebrations and marking the
beginning of the graduates' next chapter.
