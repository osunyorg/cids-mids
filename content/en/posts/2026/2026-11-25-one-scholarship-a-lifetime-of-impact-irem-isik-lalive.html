When İrem I şik received her
admission offer from the Master in International Dispute Settlement (MIDS), she
knew immediately that it was the programme she wanted to pursue.

Yet, like many talented young lawyers around the world, she faced a
difficult reality.

Studying in Geneva would require not only tuition fees but also
relocation costs, housing expenses, and the financial commitment of living in
one of Europe's most expensive cities.

Having only recently graduated and begun her professional career,
attending the programme seemed beyond reach.

Everything changed
when she learned that she had been awarded a scholarship from LALIVE .


The
scholarship was the decisive factor that enabled me to accept the MIDS
admission offer. When I
learned that I had been awarded the LALIVE Scholarship, I accepted the offer
right away.
İrem Işik
MIDS 2025 | LALIVE Scholarship Recipient


Today, İrem works
in the International Dispute Resolution Practice at Diamond McCarthy LLP in Washington,
D.C. , but she remains deeply aware of how pivotal that scholarship was in
shaping her professional journey.

More than financial support

For İrem, the value
of a scholarship extends far beyond covering tuition fees.


A scholarship
does not merely open the door to a classroom. It can open doors to international mobility,
professional networks, and experiences that might otherwise remain out of
reach.
İrem Işik
MIDS 2025 | LALIVE Scholarship Recipient


The opportunity to
study in Geneva offered far more than academic training. It provided direct
exposure to one of the world's leading hubs for international law and dispute
resolution.

During her time at
the MIDS, she attended professional events, engaged with internationally
recognised practitioners, and visited institutions that she had previously
encountered only through textbooks and academic articles, including the World
Trade Organization (WTO) , the Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA) ,
and the International Court of Justice (ICJ) .

For students coming
from jurisdictions that are geographically distant from major arbitration
centres, these opportunities can be transformative.

"The
opportunity to live and study in Geneva is not equally accessible to
everyone," she notes. "Travel
expenses, geographical distance, and visa-related challenges can all become
significant barriers."

Scholarship support
helps remove those barriers and enables talented students to fully participate
in an international professional environment.

Building a more diverse profession

İrem also sees
scholarships as an important tool for strengthening diversity and
representation within the international dispute resolution community.


I believe
that investing in talented students is essential if we want to build a more
diverse international arbitration community.
İrem Işik
MIDS 2025 | LALIVE Scholarship Recipient


For students from
regions where arbitration and international dispute settlement remain
relatively young fields, specialised training can have a particularly
significant impact.

"For students
coming from regions where the arbitration community is less established and
opportunities to gain practical exposure are more limited than in established
arbitration centres such as Geneva, the MIDS provides a valuable opportunity to
understand how international arbitration operates in practice and bring that
knowledge back to their home jurisdictions."

This perspective
reflects one of the core objectives of the MIDS scholarship programme: ensuring
that the future of international dispute settlement benefits from a wider range
of voices, experiences, and legal traditions.

A year of firsts

For İrem, the MIDS
was not only an academic milestone but also a personal one.

"MIDS
represented many first experiences," she says.

It was her first
time studying entirely in English and her first opportunity to immerse herself
in such an international academic and professional environment.

The experience
broadened her horizons and inspired new ambitions for her legal career.

"It also
inspired me to pursue professional qualification in a second jurisdiction in
addition to my home country, Türkiye." She credits both
the programme's distinguished faculty and its vibrant extracurricular
activities for helping make the year so impactful.

"These
experiences shaped me both personally and professionally, and I still feel
privileged to have been given that opportunity."

A lasting connection

Among the many
outcomes of scholarship support, one stands out in particular for İrem.

Beyond academic
achievements, professional networks, and career opportunities, scholarships
create something less tangible but equally meaningful: a lasting connection
between donors and recipients.  


The LALIVE
Scholarship has remained an important part of my journey, and I am sincerely
grateful to the firm for its investment in my education and future.
İrem Işik
MIDS 2025 | LALIVE Scholarship Recipient

A scholarship is
more than financial assistance. It is an investment in talent, potential, and
future leadership. For İrem, that
investment opened the door to Geneva, to an international professional network,
and ultimately to a career in international dispute resolution. For future
scholarship recipients, it may do the same.
