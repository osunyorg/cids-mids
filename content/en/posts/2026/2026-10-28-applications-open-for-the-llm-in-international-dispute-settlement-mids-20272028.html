Applications are now open for the 2027-2028 edition of the LL.M. in International Dispute Settlement (MIDS), one of the world's leading specialised postgraduate programmes in international arbitration, international litigation, public international law and dispute settlement. Jointly offered by the University of Geneva and the Graduate Institute of International and Development Studies, the MIDS brings together outstanding students, leading academics, judges, arbitrators, counsel and dispute settlement practitioners for an intensive year of academic and professional development in Geneva.

Located at the heart of the international legal community, the programme offers direct exposure to international organisations, arbitral institutions, governments, law firms and practitioners active across the full spectrum of international dispute settlement.

body: >-

Academic Excellence. Dispute Settlement. Professional Development.

The MIDS is
designed for candidates who have already begun building a serious interest in
dispute settlement and who are seeking to enter or accelerate a career in the
field.

Successful
applicants typically demonstrate:


Strong academic achievement;
Prior engagement with dispute resolution,
arbitration, litigation, negotiation, international adjudication, or
related fields;
A clear professional objective;
The ability to contribute to an internationally
diverse cohort.

Applicants
often bring relevant experience through internships, professional practice, research, publications, government service, participation in international moot courts, leadership and extracurricular initiatives.

Participation
in moots is highly valued as evidence of engagement with advocacy,
international law and dispute settlement, particularly when combined with
broader academic, professional, or leadership experience.

Why the MIDS?

The MIDS
offers a highly specialised alternative to a traditional generalist LL.M.

Students
benefit from:

- An academically rigorous curriculum combining
theory and practice; - Courses taught by leading practitioners and
scholars;
- Workshops and simulations focused on advocacy,
strategy and dispute settlement practice;
- Direct interaction with institutions and
practitioners at the centre of international dispute settlement;
- A uniquely international learning environment
bringing together participants from a wide range of jurisdictions,
professional backgrounds and legal traditions;
Dedicated career support and networking
opportunities throughout the year.

The
programme is specifically designed for candidates seeking to develop both
substantive expertise and professional readiness for careers in arbitration,
international litigation, public international law, investment arbitration,
trade disputes and related fields.

MIDS
graduates have gone on to careers in leading international law firms, arbitral
institutions, international organisations, governments, courts and tribunals,
academia and the private sector across the world.

Learn more about the MIDS advantage
https://mids.ch/admissions/the-mids-advantage/

Why Geneva?

 Geneva
offers a unique environment for the study and practice of international dispute
settlement.

Home to
international organisations, diplomatic missions, arbitral institutions, NGOs
and leading law firms, the city provides students with unparalleled
opportunities to engage with the international legal community throughout the
year.

Studying in
Geneva allows MIDS students to develop their expertise in a setting where
international law, diplomacy and dispute settlement are part of everyday
professional life.

Is the MIDS right for you?
 

A strong
MIDS application typically answers four questions:


Why dispute settlement?
Why the MIDS?
Why now?
How does the programme fit within your
professional trajectory?

Applicants
who already hold an LL.M. in arbitration or dispute resolution are welcome to
apply. However, they should clearly explain the additional academic or
professional value they expect to derive from the MIDS in light of their prior
studies and experience.
