What does international arbitration look like beyond the classroom?

For 35 MIDS students, this year's Paris Study Trip provided a unique opportunity to find out firsthand through direct engagement with the ICC International Court of Arbitration, leading global law firms, renowned arbitration practitioners, and the wider international arbitration community.

The trip began with an alumni and friends reception in Paris, reconnecting current students with graduates and practitioners working across the field, demonstrating why experiential learning remains a cornerstone of the MIDS experience.

body: >-

Inside the ICC
A highlight of the week was an intensive programme at the headquarters of the
ICC International Court of Arbitration , led by Alexander Fessas , Secretary General of the ICC Court and Director of ICC Dispute Resolution Services.
Students gained valuable insights into the practical operation of international arbitration, exploring topics such as efficiency in arbitral proceedings, scrutiny of draft awards, and the work of the ICC Secretariat. The sessions offered a rare behind-the-scenes
perspective on one of the world's most influential arbitral institutions.

Learning from arbitration leaders
The programme continued at Clifford Chance Paris , where students engaged with some of the most respected figures in the field, including
Professor Pierre Tercier , Simon Greenberg , Jason Fry KC ,
Marie Stoyanov , Emmanuel Jolivet , Galina Zukova , Carole Malinvaud , and
Fernando Mantilla-Serrano .
Discussions addressed some of the most pressing issues facing arbitration today, from due process and sanctions to the role of States in international disputes and the future of the profession.

Building the next generation of arbitration professionals

The study trip concluded with participation in the 46th ICC Institute of World Business Law Annual Conference , allowing students to engage with the latest developments and debates shaping international dispute resolution. The Paris Study Trip illustrates what makes the MIDS experience distinctive: unparalleled access to leading institutions, direct interaction with world-class practitioners, and opportunities to build an international professional network
from day one.
It is also a reminder of the value of investing in the next generation of international dispute settlement professionals. Our sincere thanks to the ICC, Clifford Chance, our speakers, alumni, and friends of the programme for making this exceptional experience possible.
