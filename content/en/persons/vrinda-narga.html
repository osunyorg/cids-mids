Vrinda Nargas is an India-qualified lawyer with three years of experience in international commercial arbitration and commercial litigation. She holds a B.A., LL.B. (Business Law Hons.) from National Law University, Jodhpur (India), where she served as Editor-in-Chief of the NLUJ Law Review. She has taken part in national and international arbitration and ADR competitions, and regularly contributes to legal writing.

Before joining the MIDS programme, Vrinda was an associate in the dispute resolution practice at Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, a GAR 100-ranked firm. Her work centred on representing and advising clients in ad hoc and institutional arbitrations seated in India and Singapore, arising from sectors such as energy, oil and gas, consumer goods and construction. She was also involved in complex commercial litigation before the High Court of Delhi and the Supreme Court of India. In addition, she was seconded to the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, where she worked on technology policy and data privacy regulation, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

Beyond her professional pursuits, Vrinda takes a keen interest in languages. She is a native speaker of English and Hindi, fluent in French, and currently developing her proficiency in Spanish.
