Ana Leticia Paranhos is a dual-qualified lawyer admitted to practice in Brazil and Mexico, specializing in international arbitration, cross-border transactions, and international contracts. She has over a decade of experience advising public and private companies and assisting arbitral tribunals in complex, high-value commercial and investment disputes across Europe, North America, and Latin America. She has also served as sole arbitrator in construction disputes seated in Mexico.

Sector Experience: Construction, infrastructure, energy, mining, commodities, shipping, and services.
