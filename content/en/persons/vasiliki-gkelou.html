Vasiliki is an LL.M. candidate in International Dispute Settlement (MIDS).

She holds an LL.M. in International Commercial Arbitration from Democritus University of Thrace, where she graduated with a summa cum laude distinction. Her master’s thesis was entitled “Alternative Dispute Resolution as a Contractual Pre-Arbitration Requirement.” During her undergraduate law studies, she also attended the Summer Intensive Programme on European Private Law at the University of Salzburg.

Vasiliki is a Greek-qualified lawyer with three and a half years of professional experience in commercial litigation, banking, consumer law, and contract law. She has gained practical experience in commercial disputes through her work at a boutique law firm and a law office in Thessaloniki, Greece.
