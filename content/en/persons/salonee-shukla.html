Salonee Shukla is an India-qualified lawyer with three years of post-qualification experience at the intersection of international economic law and commercial dispute resolution. She began her career in international trade remedies, representing foreign exporters in anti-dumping and countervailing duty investigations. She subsequently practised commercial litigation and arbitration in New Delhi, appearing before the Supreme Court of India, various High Courts and specialised company law tribunals. Her work focused on arbitration, insolvency and disputes arising out of complex domestic and cross-border commercial transactions.

Along with her practice before Indian courts and tribunals, Salonee served as the Secretary and Global Coordinator of Geneva-based TradeLab Legal Clinics, an international network of university-based legal clinics working in international economic law. She also independently advised Indian businesses on international trade regulations and represented clients in arbitration, arbitration-related litigation and mediation proceedings.

Salonee graduated with a B.A., LL.B. (Hons.) from Gujarat National Law University in 2023 and undertook an academic exchange at Leiden Law School in the Netherlands. During her studies, she held research assistantship positions, contributing to publications on international economic law. She also represented her university at the World Rounds of the John H. Jackson Moot Court Competition and subsequently coached teams that also qualified for the World Rounds.

Beyond the law, Salonee spends her time visiting museums, watching true crime documentaries and listening to Bollywood music.
