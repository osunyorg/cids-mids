Emma is a French-qualified lawyer. Prior to joining the MIDS, she worked for three years as an associate in the competition law team of one of the leading law firms in France – Darrois Villey Maillot Brochier.

She worked on cases relating to a broad range of competition law matters, including merger control, competition litigation and follow-on damages claims. Her practice encompassed both advisory work and representation before French and EU competition authorities and courts. She has advised clients in sectors including digital services, retail, banking and insurance, telecommunications and IT services.

Before joining Darrois Villey Maillot Brochier, Emma completed internships at BDGS and Latham & Watkins in Paris as part of her professional legal training. She also interned in the economic and financial regulation chamber of the Paris Court of Appeal.

Emma obtained her Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) from the University of Rennes 1 (mention bien). She also holds a Master’s degree in International and European Law from Toulouse Capitole University (mention bien), a Master’s degree in Economic Law of the European Union from Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University (mention bien) and an LL.M. from King’s College London (with merit).
