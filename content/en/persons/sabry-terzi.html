Sabry is a French- and Luxembourg-qualified lawyer with over five years of experience across international tax, banking, capital markets and structured finance.

Prior to joining the MIDS, Sabry worked as an associate in the Capital Markets & Banking team of Linklaters LLP in Luxembourg and subsequently in the structured and trade finance practice of a fintech company in London. His experience includes advising international clients on complex cross-border financing transactions, including private equity and M&A financings, structured finance and restructuring matters, as well as related security and enforcement issues.

Building on his background in international finance, Sabry is developing his expertise in international dispute settlement, with a particular interest in commercial and investment arbitration and disputes arising from economic sanctions.

Sabry holds a double degree in Business Contract Law and Management from SKEMA Business School and Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France. He is a member of Young ICCA.
