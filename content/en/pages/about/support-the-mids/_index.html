Every year, exceptional lawyers from around the world earn admission to the Master in International Dispute Settlement (MIDS). Yet many are unable to take up their place because of financial barriers. Scholarships ensure that talent, commitment, and potential, rather than financial means, determine who can access one of the world's leading programmes in international dispute settlement.

By supporting a MIDS scholarship, donors invest not only in an individual student, but also in the future capacity, diversity, and legitimacy of the global dispute resolution community.

Support a scholarship. Open a door to opportunity.

The impact of scholarship support
Since 2008:

More than 190 students have benefited from MIDS-managed scholarship support
Scholarship recipients have come from every major world region
Alumni now work in international organisations, governments, universities, arbitral institutions, international courts, NGOs and leading law firms worldwide

Scholarships do more than support individual careers. They strengthen institutions, expand access to legal expertise, and contribute to the peaceful resolution of disputes around the world.

Strengthening global representation
International dispute settlement increasingly affects developing and emerging economies. Yet access to specialist training remains uneven.
Scholarships help ensure that talented lawyers from underrepresented regions can participate in shaping the future of the field and bring this expertise back to their institutions, communities and jurisdictions.

Opportunities for support
Illustrative giving levels

Donors may support full scholarship, partial scholarships, named scholarships and multi-year scholarship fund. The MIDS can also develop bespoke arrangements aligned with a donor's geographic priorities, thematic interests, or philanthropic objectives.

Strong governance and stewardship
The MIDS is committed to ensuring that scholarship funding is allocated fairly, transparently and effectively.
Scholarships are awarded on the basis of:

Academic excellence
Demonstrated financial need
Diversity and inclusion considerations

The scholarship framework includes verification procedures, transparent allocation mechanisms, safeguards against duplicate funding, and regular impact reporting.
