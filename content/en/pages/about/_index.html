What is the MIDS?

An accredited, top-tier  Master of Advanced Studies (LL.M.)  programme, the MIDS stands for "Master in International Dispute Settlement." Offered jointly by the University of Geneva Faculty of Law and the Geneva Graduate Institute of International and Development Studies , it delivers advanced and specialized legal training in international dispute settlement.

The degree awarded upon successful completion of the programme is the Master of Advanced Studies (LL.M.) in International Dispute Settlement. It carries 90 ECTS credits and is classified as a Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) within the Swiss higher education system. Recognition of the degree for professional, academic, or doctoral admission purposes is determined by the competent authorities and institutions in the relevant jurisdiction.
